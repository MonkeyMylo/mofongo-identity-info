Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026.

Information accessed and purpose

When you sign in, Mofongo Home receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. The family's apps use your email address to check that you are on the invited list and to show who made a change. They use nothing else from your Google account.

Storage

Sign-in is handled on the family's own computers on its home network. A signed session cookie keeps you signed in on your device. Your email address may be stored with the records you create in an app, such as who added an entry. Nothing is stored on this website.

Sharing and use limitations

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, published, or shared with analytics providers or other third parties. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and control

You can revoke Mofongo Home's access at any time in your Google Account settings, under third-party connections. Ask the family member who invited you to remove your data from an app.

Limited Use

Mofongo Home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for privacy questions.
